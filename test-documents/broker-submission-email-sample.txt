In addition to all unknown risks coverage, we are requesting specific risk coverage for the following matters identified in the legal due diligence report:

1. ABSENCE OF DRAINAGE EASEMENT
The Property has drained into the Thames Water public sewer beneath Harbour Exchange Square since at least 1979 (47 years) without any formal grant of easement. Squire Patton Boggs have confirmed that the 20-year prescriptive period has been met but no formal documentation exists. We request specific insurance coverage for the risk that a claim is made against the Buyer arising from the absence of a formal drainage easement.

2. POSSESSORY TITLE — NORTH WING (approx. 6,200 sq ft)
The north wing of the Property was registered with possessory title in 1987 following destruction of title deeds in a fire. The title has not been upgraded to absolute despite 38 years of possession. We request specific coverage for the risk that a prior owner or their successor emerges and claims title to the north wing.

3. BREACH OF RESTRICTIVE COVENANT
The title is subject to a 1962 restrictive covenant restricting use to offices only. The ground floor is currently let to Costa Coffee in breach of this covenant. The original beneficiary company was dissolved in 1998. We request coverage for the risk that the benefit of the covenant has passed to a successor who enforces it.
